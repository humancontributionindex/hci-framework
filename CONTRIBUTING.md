# Contributing to the Human Contribution Index

Thank you for your interest in contributing to the HCI framework. This is an open, community-driven project and we welcome contributions from researchers, educators, and practitioners across all disciplines.

## Ways to Contribute

### 1. Improve the Rubric
- Suggest clearer behavioral anchors
- Identify edge cases or ambiguities in scoring criteria
- Propose additional evidence indicators

### 2. Disciplinary Calibration
- Help adapt the framework for specific academic fields
- Provide field-specific examples for scoring anchors
- Share insights on how human contribution manifests differently across disciplines

### 3. Translation
- Translate the rubric into other languages
- Ensure translated versions maintain the precision of the scoring criteria

### 4. Validation
- Apply the rubric to research in your field and share your experience
- Contribute scored assessments (anonymized) to help build validation datasets
- Report inter-rater reliability findings

### 5. Case Studies
- Document your experience using the HCI
- Share lessons learned from implementation in your institution

## How to Contribute

1. **Open an Issue** describing your proposed change or contribution
2. **Fork the repository** and create a branch for your changes
3. **Submit a Pull Request** with a clear description of what you've changed and why
4. **Engage in discussion** — we review all PRs and may suggest refinements

## Code of Conduct

- Be respectful and constructive
- Ground suggestions in evidence and reasoning
- Acknowledge that research quality manifests differently across disciplines
- Focus on improving the framework, not criticizing specific research
